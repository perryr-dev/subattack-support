Gameplay Help

Launch torpedoes to sink enemy ships. Choose Classic Mode for traditional arcade gameplay or Arcade Mode for combos, special weapons and bonus challenges.

Use the Options menu inside the game to adjust sound and gameplay settings.

Premium Purchases

Sub Attack! Premium is an optional one-time in-app purchase that removes advertising and unlocks exclusive Arcade ocean themes.

If you've previously purchased Premium, open the game's Options menu and select Restore Purchases.

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple or Google account used for the original purchase.

Report a Problem

When contacting support, please include:

  • Your device model
  • Your iOS or Android version
  • A description of the problem
  • A screenshot, if helpful

Please do not send passwords or payment card details.
